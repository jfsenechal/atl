  Good to know
  - If the quota runs out during a run, the worker waits for the next hour and
    can go past 60 minutes, so two workers may briefly run at once. That's
    harmless: the queue doesn't give the same mail to two workers. If OVH kills
    the task while it's waiting, the mail it had picked up is sent again after
    one hour; it isn't lost.

    same OVH account also send mail, lower MAILER_HOURLY_LIMIT.
